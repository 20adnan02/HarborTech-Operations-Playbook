# Week 2 Project Charter Draft

## Project Title
RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System Replacement

## Project Sponsor
Marcus Webb

## Business Need
Riverside Community Health Network needs to replace its aging patient intake and scheduling system used across six clinics. The replacement is needed to provide a more reliable platform, improve consistency in intake and scheduling processes, and better align clinic workflows with organizational needs.

## Purpose and High-Level Scope
The purpose of this project is to replace RCHN's aging patient intake and scheduling system with a solution that better supports operations across its six clinics. The high-level scope includes reviewing current intake and scheduling workflows, identifying stakeholder needs, defining requirements for the replacement system, and establishing clear project boundaries before detailed planning begins.

## Success Criteria
1. A replacement patient intake and scheduling system supports the agreed needs of all six RCHN clinics.
2. Intake and scheduling processes are more consistent across the clinics.
3. The new system provides a reliable platform that aligns with clinic workflows and supports agreed patient service goals.
